Our Services

Complete, personalised support at every stage of your study-abroad journey.

University Selection

We help you shortlist universities and programmes that truly fit your academic profile, budget, and career goals, so you apply with confidence, not guesswork.

Admissions Assistance

From application forms and statements of purpose to organising documents and deadlines, we make sure your application is complete, polished, and submitted on time.

Student Visa Guidance

Step-by-step support with your student visa, paperwork, financial documentation, appointment booking, and interview preparation to maximise your chances.

Scholarship Support

We identify scholarships and funding you qualify for and guide you through each application, helping make studying abroad more affordable.

Career Counselling

Honest one-to-one counselling to help you choose a course and country aligned with your long-term ambitions and career path.

Pre-Departure Guidance

Accommodation advice, travel checklists, and settling-in tips so that when you land, you feel prepared, safe, and confident.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a consultation cost?

Your first consultation is completely free. We'll assess your profile, answer your questions, and outline your options with no obligation.

Which countries do you help with?

We currently specialise in student visas and admissions for France and Italy, along with admissions and scholarship guidance. Contact us to discuss your specific goals.

Do you help with scholarships?

Yes. We identify scholarships and funding opportunities you qualify for and guide you through the application process to make studying abroad more affordable.

When should I start the process?

The earlier the better, ideally several months before your intended intake, so there's plenty of time for admissions, documents, and the visa. Reach out and we'll map a realistic timeline for you.
