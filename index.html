Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions students ask us most. Still unsure? Just reach out, the first chat is free.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation is completely free. We assess your profile, answer your questions, and outline realistic options, with no obligation.

Which countries do you help with?

We specialise in student admissions, visas, and scholarships for France and Italy, two of Europe's most affordable, high-quality study destinations. Exploring elsewhere? Tell us your goal and we'll advise.

Can I study in France or Italy in English?

Absolutely. Both countries offer a wide and growing range of English-taught bachelor's and master's degrees, so you don't necessarily need to speak French or Italian to apply.

Do you help with scholarships?

Yes. We identify scholarships and funding you qualify for, such as the Eiffel Excellence Scholarship in France or regional and university grants in Italy, and guide you through each application.

When should I start the process?

The earlier the better, ideally several months before your intended intake. That leaves enough time for admissions, documents, and the student visa without rushing.

How do I get started?

Fill in the enquiry form, or call/WhatsApp us at +92 321 5208625. We'll reply promptly and map out your personalised path.
